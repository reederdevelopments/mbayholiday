About MBayHoliday

Connecting travellers and locals with authentic experiences across South Africa's Garden Route & Klein Karoo.

Our Story & Local Roots

MBayHoliday (mbayholiday.co.za) was born out of a simple observation on the Southern Cape coast: while thousands of holidaymakers and weekend travellers visit Mossel Bay, Hartenbos, Great Brak River, George, Wilderness, Sedgefield, Knysna, Plettenberg Bay, and Oudtshoorn every month, the best local happenings—Saturday morning boeremarkte, church bazaars, live acoustic sunset sessions, charity trail runs, and family farm days—were scattered across dozens of separate posters and community groups.

Developed by Reeder Developments in partnership with Du Macha Verhurings, and enriched by local hosts from the Garden Route Weekend Trips, Adventures & Hidden Gems community, MBayHoliday brings these authentic local experiences together into one fast, free, interactive trip-planning platform.

How We Curate & Verify Listings

Unlike automated scraper directories, every event, activity, weekend market, and restaurant on MBayHoliday is human-reviewed before publication. For each listing, our team works to verify:

  • Accurate Dates, Times & Pricing: Clear breakdowns of entry fees, free community access, or tiered ticket options in South African Rand (ZAR).
  • Pinpoint Geolocation: Verified GPS coordinates so visitors can jump straight to our interactive Garden Route map or open turn-by-turn directions.
  • Weather & Vibe Suitability: Practical tags indicating whether an activity is outdoor, indoor/rain-safe, family-friendly, or pet-friendly.
  • Direct Host Connection: We link directly to official event organisers, telephone numbers, and WhatsApp booking lines without charging travellers any middleman commission.

Supporting Local Small Businesses & Non-Profits

Standard listings for community markets, charity fundraisers, local artists, activities, and restaurants are completely free to submit via our Add Listing page. Business owners can also update or remove their information at any time through our Manage Listing portal. Optional featured sponsorships and display advertising help cover our hosting, verification, and development costs so the platform remains free for all travellers.
