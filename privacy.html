Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to MBayHoliday (https://mbayholiday.co.za). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and international privacy standards (including GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

  • Voluntary Submissions: When you submit a business or event via our Add Listing or Manage Listing forms, your browser prepares an email using your default email client. We only receive the contact details (such as name, business phone, email address, and listing details) that you actively choose to send us.
  • Browser Local Storage ("My Trip List"): When you star events, activities, or restaurants to build your itinerary, those selections are saved locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage (key: mbay_stars). This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Optional Geolocation ("Near Me" Map Feature): If you click the "Near Me" button on our interactive map, your browser will ask your permission to access your approximate location to center the map. Your coordinates are processed solely within your browser and are never stored or logged by MBayHoliday.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that help keep MBayHoliday free for travellers and local communities. Please note the following required disclosures regarding how Google uses data when you visit our site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to mbayholiday.co.za and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website integrates public map tiles from OpenStreetMap (via Leaflet) and live Mossel Bay weather data from the Open-Meteo API. Listings also contain external links to event organisers, ticketing platforms (such as Quicket, Webtickets, or Howler), Google Maps, and WhatsApp. MBayHoliday is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

4. Your Rights & Contact Information

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal or business listing information held on MBayHoliday. To request an immediate update or removal, please use our Manage Listing page or email us directly at reederdevelopments@gmail.com.
