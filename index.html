Editorial Destination Guide

Planning Your Garden Route & Mossel Bay Holiday

Essential local knowledge for exploring South Africa's scenic Southern Cape coastline.

Read Full Town-by-Town Guides

📍 Mossel Bay, Hartenbos & Great Brak

Known for one of the mildest year-round climates on Earth, Mossel Bay combines Blue Flag swimming at Santos and Diaz beaches with the dramatic rock pools and 1.1km over-ocean zipline at The Point. Just up the R102, Hartenbos and Great Brak River offer tranquil estuaries, historic village bridges, Saturday morning boeremarkte, and malaria-free Big Five game drives at Botlierskop.

Read Mossel Bay & Brak River Guide →

📍 George, Wilderness & Sedgefield

Framed by the towering Outeniqua Mountains, George is home to Redberry Farm's strawberry fields and giant hedge maze, the 150-stall Outeniqua Family Market, and world-class mountain trails. Ten minutes east, Wilderness and Cittaslow town Sedgefield invite you to canoe the Touws River to a forest waterfall, visit rescued donkeys in Wilderness Heights, or dance at a Myoli Beach sunset session.

Read George & Lakes District Guide →

📍 Knysna, Plett & Oudtshoorn

Explore the sandstone cliffs of the Knysna Heads, hike the iconic Robberg Nature Reserve peninsula in Plettenberg Bay alongside seals and dolphins, or tackle forest e-bike trails at Maple Ranch. Whenever coastal clouds roll in, drive 50 minutes inland over the Outeniqua Pass to sunny Oudtshoorn in the Klein Karoo to marvel at the ancient limestone halls of the Cango Caves.

Frequently Asked Questions About Visiting the Garden Route

What is the best time of year to visit Mossel Bay and the Garden Route?

The Garden Route is a true year-round destination. December to February brings warm 21°C ocean water and major summer festivals; March to May offers calm, windless autumn hiking weather; June to October is peak Southern Right whale-watching season from the coastal cliffs; and September to November showcases spring fynbos blooms.

What can families do on a rainy or windy day on the Garden Route?

Tap the ☔ Rainy Day / Indoor filter pill at the top of our directory! Top weather-proof favourites include browsing The Goods Shed Indoor Market or the Bartolomeu Dias Maritime Museum in Mossel Bay, joining an artisanal cheese-making or resin craft workshop, or taking a 50-minute drive over the Outeniqua mountains to Oudtshoorn's Cango Caves.

Which weekend markets happen every Saturday and Sunday?

On Saturdays, don't miss the Outeniqua Family Market in George, Midbrak Mark in Great Brak River, Brinkleys Shed in Little Brak River, Hartenbos Boeremark, and The Goods Shed in Mossel Bay. On Sundays, head to the Milkwood Village Sunday Market in Wilderness or the monthly Sunday Social Market at Trail Kiosk.
