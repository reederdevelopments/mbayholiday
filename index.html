How to use MBayHoliday

Save Your Favorites

Click the star icon on any activity or event to add it to 'My List'. You can view your saved itinerary anytime by clicking 'My List' at the top, and even share it with friends via WhatsApp!

Find What You Need

Use the Filters button to search by town, category, date, or price. You can also filter by places that are "Open Now". Once you enter your preferences, click Apply Filters to update.

Switch Views

Use the tabs at the top to switch between visual Cards, a compact List, a Calendar layout, local Dining spots, or the interactive Map view.
